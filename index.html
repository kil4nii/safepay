Event log
    Demo script
    1. Buyer creates an order with two milestones and funds it. The money goes to escrow, not to the supplier.
    2. Supplier sees the order is funded, delivers milestone 1 and uploads proof.
    3. Optional: supplier takes an early payout from the financing partner (1.5% fee).
    4. Buyer approves (money released) or disputes (money frozen). If the buyer does nothing, escrow auto-releases after 72 hours.
    5. Delivery checkout: a customer prepays a small shop into escrow. The courier hands over the parcel and the customer's 4-digit code releases the money. No cash on delivery.
    6. API & webhooks: how any platform plugs SafePay in.
